# Biology pack: roles and colors

Check every frame in greyscale: the roles must still read.

## Heroes: immune cells

White body, a colored rim that is fixed for each type, firm eyes with brows.

| Type | Sprite | Rim |
|---|---|---|
| NK cell | `nk` | blue |
| CD8 killer T cell | `killer_t` | green |
| CD4 helper T cell | `helper_t` | orange |
| Regulatory T cell | `treg` | lavender |
| Dendritic cell | `dendritic` | pink |
| Macrophage | `macrophage` | brown |
| B cell | `b_cell` | dark green |

## Villains: tumor cells

Plum body, a solid red rim, black outline, angry brows. Red is for villains only.

## Civilians: normal body cells

Soft warm tones (peach, pink), dot eyes, no brows.

## Molecules

When the story is about molecules that touch, show them as parts on the cell surface in a `contact` scene,
with the links of the plan's `model:`. Badges above a cell are for wide shots that only name a cell type.
Colors of parts: MHC class I yellow (yellow is reserved for it), TCR green, PD-1 light grey, PD-L1 pink,
CTLA-4 lavender, CD80 peach, antibodies blue.

## Rules

- Death caused by an immune cell is apoptosis (shrink and break up), never an explosion.
- No real person, logo, or brand. Patients have no names.

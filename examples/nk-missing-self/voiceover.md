# Voice-over: How NK cells find cancer cells

## S01
Missing self. How natural killer cells find cancer cells.

## S02
Your body has natural killer cells, or NK cells. They patrol the tissues and check each cell that they meet.

## S03
A healthy cell shows MHC class one on its surface. It works like an ID badge. The NK cell reads the badge and moves on.

## S04
Some cancer cells drop this badge. Then killer T cells cannot see them.

## S05
But for an NK cell, a missing badge is the alarm. It attacks, and the cancer cell dies.

## S06
Badge present: spare the cell. Badge missing: kill it. This is the missing self rule.

# The Higgs boson: why particles have mass

<!-- Read at about 2.7 words a second. One heading for each scene of explainer.yaml. -->

## S01
Why do particles have mass?

## S02
Space is filled with an invisible field: the Higgs field.

## S03
A photon does not interact with it, so it has no mass.

## S04
An electron interacts a little, so it gets a small mass.

## S05
A top quark interacts strongly, so it is very heavy.

## S06
To test this idea, physicists smashed protons together at the LHC.

## S07
The crash can shake the field and make a ripple: a Higgs boson.

## S08
It lives for a tiny moment, then decays, for example into two photons.

## S09
In 2012, the ATLAS and CMS experiments saw it, at a mass of about 125 GeV.

## S10
The Higgs boson is a ripple of the field that gives elementary particles their mass.
